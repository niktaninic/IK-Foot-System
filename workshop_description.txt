[h1]IK Foot System[/h1]
[i]Feet that actually stand on the ground.[/i]

In vanilla GMod the ground is more of a suggestion. Feet float over stairs, sink into slopes and the walk animation happily skips three steps at a time.

IK Foot System fixes that with real-time inverse kinematics: every foot is placed on what is really under it, the knees bend to reach it and the body follows the terrain. Less levitation, more walking.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Real stair walking[/b] - one tread per step, alternating feet, lifting over each edge going up and down. Run fast enough and it takes two at a time, like a person would.
[*][b]Smooth body on stairs[/b] - no more popping up a whole step at once; the body follows the staircase and crouches only as much as the feet need.
[*][b]Slopes, curbs and props[/b] - planted feet stay put, turn to lie flat on the surface and don't slide around.
[*][b]Exact leg IK[/b] - a proper two-bone solver, the foot lands exactly where it should.
[*][b]Works with your model[/b] - leg bones found automatically (also on non-ValveBiped rigs), every model measured once, shoes and skirts on odd bones follow the feet, hair and helper bones are never touched.
[*][b]PAC3 compatible[/b] - works on top of PAC parts and other bone-changing addons.
[*][b]Everyone gets it[/b] - other players get IK with their own settings. Limit it by distance or turn it off for performance.
[*][b]Edge cases handled[/b] - ledges aren't mistaken for stairs, invisible stair clips are ignored, teleports and respawns reset cleanly.
[/list]

[h2]Settings menu[/h2]
Open with [b]ik_foot_menu[/b], [b]!ikfoot[/b] / [b]/ikfoot[/b] in chat, or [b]Spawn menu > Utilities > User > IK Foot[/b].

[list]
[*][b]Quick setup[/b] - the main switches and four styles: Default, Subtle, Responsive, Grounded.
[*][b]Ground, Legs & feet, Stairs, Body, Automation[/b] - every option explained in plain words, with a reset button.
[*][b]Search[/b] - find any setting by name.
[*][b]Performance[/b] - IK on other players and up to what distance.
[*][b]Presets[/b] - save and load your own setups.
[*][b]Diagnostics[/b] - debug overlay and bug report tools.
[/list]

The defaults are tuned to work out of the box - most people never need to open the menu.

[h2]Installation[/h2]
Subscribe and play. Works in singleplayer and on servers that have the addon installed (the server relays everyone's settings).

[h2]Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]ik_foot 0/1[/b] - turn the system off / on
[*][b]ik_foot_menu[/b] - settings menu
[*][b]ik_foot_hard_reset[/b] - clear all IK state and start over
[*][b]ik_foot_debug 0/1/2[/b] - debug overlay
[*][b]ik_foot_diag[/b] - short report in the console, with likely causes
[*][b]ik_foot_dump[/b] - save a full report file for bug reports
[*][b]ik_foot_capture 10[/b] - record 10 seconds for bug reports
[/list]
All settings and their console names are listed on GitHub (link below).

[h2]Something wrong?[/h2]
[b]Feet float or sink a little[/b] - adjust [i]Sole offset[/i] in the Ground page.
[b]Feet ignore tall steps[/b] - raise [i]Trace start height[/i] or [i]Ground search depth[/i].
[b]Jitter while standing[/b] - try the [i]Subtle[/i] style or raise [i]Standing speed[/i].
[b]Broken after a model change or another addon[/b] - [b]ik_foot_hard_reset[/b]. Still broken? [b]ik_foot 0[/b], switch model and back, [b]ik_foot 1[/b].

[b]Reporting a bug:[/b] run [b]ik_foot_dump[/b] right after it happens and post the file (garrysmod/data/ik_foot/) together with the map and the player model. The dump keeps the seconds before serious problems, so it shows what went wrong even a bit later.

[i]Some maps have broken collisions or cursed geometry. The addon does its best, but it's still the Source engine. If something looks cursed, it probably is.[/i]

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*]Sprinting up very steep, narrow stairs can leave a foot short of its step for a moment.
[*]In multiplayer the server must have the addon installed; on servers without it, it does nothing.
[/list]
